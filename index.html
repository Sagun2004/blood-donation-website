Why It Matters

Every 2 Seconds, Someone
Needs Blood

Donating blood is the simplest act of humanity. One donation can save up to three lives — and it costs you nothing but 30 minutes.

💉

Safe & Quick

The entire donation process takes under 30 minutes. Medically supervised, painless, and completely safe for healthy adults.

🫀

Saves 3 Lives

A single unit of blood is separated into red cells, plasma, and platelets — each component helps a different patient.

🩹

No Substitute

Blood cannot be manufactured. Accident victims, surgery patients, and cancer patients rely entirely on volunteer donors.

🔄

Replenishes in 56 Days

Your body regenerates the donated blood within 56 days. You can donate up to 6 times a year.

🏥

Local Impact

Blood donated in Dehradun stays in Dehradun — supporting local hospitals, trauma centers, and maternity wards.

🎓

Student Leadership

Students from Dehradun colleges lead drives, earn volunteer certificates, and inspire a culture of community care.

Before You Donate

Eligibility Criteria

Make sure you meet these criteria to donate safely. When in doubt, our on-site medical team will guide you.

✅ You CAN Donate If…

  • You are 18-65 years old
  • Your weight is 45 kg or more
  • You are in good general health
  • Haemoglobin ≥ 12.5 g/dL
  • Your pulse is 60-100 bpm
  • BP is 100/60 - 160/100 mmHg
  • It has been 56+ days since last donation
  • You have had a meal 4+ hours ago

❌ You CANNOT Donate If…

  • You have fever or flu-like symptoms
  • You are pregnant or recently delivered
  • You are on antibiotics or steroids
  • You have HIV, Hepatitis B/C, or malaria
  • You donated blood in the last 56 days
  • You had surgery or tattoo in last 6 months
  • You consumed alcohol in the last 24 hours
  • You have a history of heart disease
